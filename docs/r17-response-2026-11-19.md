## What changes for integrators

- Read `open_conditions` on an effect to see the exact step that keeps it open, and the record that states it.
- A REST step can return `rest_resume` instead of `rest_next_call`. Supply the listed items, then send `resume_call`.
- Read `route_audit_result.verification` for reviewer verification, beside `route_audit_result.controlling`.
- `/v1/explain` returns per-action requirements from a live effect decision as returned.

## What this does not establish

- Your gates are unproven until your frozen cohort and audit cases are rerun.
- The reader is deterministic English rules. Exclusion wordings ("gives no authority for contact") are held rather than read as scope, and new wordings may miss. Every miss holds, and none is a false favourable.
- Receivers here are AMOS-operated. The model comparison and the commercial pilot remain gated under your criteria.
