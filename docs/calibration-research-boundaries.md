# Calibration research and integrity boundaries

Minimum Viable Relationships (MVR), originated by Farouk Mark Mukiibi, African Market OS.

## What the checks establish

Calibration-health attestation checks the configured manifest inventory, asset schemas, declared approval metadata, freshness policy, and content hashes. It does not independently authenticate underlying sources, reviewer identity, scientific accuracy, or outcomes. `active_manifest_attestation_status` and `full_corpus_attested` are separate signals. A passing active subset does not clear quarantined records.

Reproducible research and passing synthetic regression cases can establish implementation consistency. They cannot reconstruct unobserved historical actuals, confer real-world permission, or establish empirical calibration. A generated hash is not evidence that a source observation happened.

## API handling

- Explicit held sectors cannot inherit an unrelated configuration simply by supplying a broader archetype. Held reference benchmarks cannot use aliases to bypass quarantine.
- An existing unapproved or pending manifest is unavailable for advisory use as well as strict use.
- A lab-labelled calibration does not gain decision-use authorization from freshness. A benchmark identified as lab research or market-level research observations does not publish an entity percentile or sampling confidence interval. Strict benchmark mode declines that reference class.
- Legacy `empirical_kv` storage labels and arbitrary version names do not establish empirical provenance. Preserve `calibration_basis.basis_verification` and its storage-source limitation when citing a configured basis. An explicit basis label is a declaration, not an independent source validation.
- Decision support confidence is not evidence completeness, a calibrated probability of success, or execution permission. Preserve the existing confidence and authorization disclosures.

## Replacing a held asset

Retain its original artifact, source claims, approval history, and quarantine reason. Either reconstruct and independently review the actual source chain or propose a new, clearly distinguished model with a defensible method and appropriately limited claims. Obtain authorized review of the exact version and scope before any separate production restoration. Do not relabel country-level proxy observations as a sample of entities, replace a missing dataset with invented actuals, or treat owner signoff as scientific validation.
