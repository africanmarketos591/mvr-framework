# Calibration research and integrity boundaries

Minimum Viable Relationships (MVR), originated by Farouk Mark Mukiibi, African Market OS.

## What the checks establish

Calibration-health attestation checks the configured manifest inventory, asset schemas, declared approval metadata, freshness policy, and content hashes. It does not independently authenticate underlying sources, reviewer identity, scientific accuracy, or outcomes. `active_manifest_attestation_status` and `full_corpus_attested` are separate signals. A passing active subset does not clear quarantined records.

Reproducible research and passing synthetic regression cases can establish implementation consistency. They cannot reconstruct unobserved historical actuals, confer real-world permission, or establish empirical calibration. A generated hash is not evidence that a source observation happened.

## API handling

- Explicit held sectors cannot inherit an unrelated configuration simply by supplying a broader archetype. Held reference benchmarks cannot use aliases to bypass quarantine.
- An existing unapproved or pending manifest is unavailable for advisory use as well as strict use.
- A lab-labelled calibration does not gain decision-use authorization from freshness. A benchmark identified as lab research or market-level research observations does not publish an entity percentile or sampling confidence interval. Strict benchmark mode declines that reference class.
- Legacy `empirical_kv` storage labels and arbitrary version names do not establish empirical provenance. Preserve `calibration_basis.basis_verification` and its storage-source limitation when citing a configured basis. An explicit basis label is a declaration, not an independent source validation.
- Decision support confidence is not evidence completeness, a calibrated probability of success, or execution permission. Preserve the existing confidence and authorization disclosures.

## Replacing a held asset

Retain its original artifact, source claims, approval history, and quarantine reason. Either reconstruct and independently review the actual source chain or propose a new, clearly distinguished model with a defensible method and appropriately limited claims. Obtain authorized review of the exact version and scope before any separate production restoration. Do not relabel country-level proxy observations as a sample of entities, replace a missing dataset with invented actuals, or treat owner signoff as scientific validation.

The October 1, 2026 lab proposal remains research-only, not an approved replacement. Its synthetic test approvals are not live owner approvals. No asset has been restored on the strength of that proposal.

## Governed retirement

Release `2026-10-01.calibration-disposition.1` supports the separately authorized
retirement decision `CAL-RET-2026-10-001`. Its reason is provenance unverified
after scoped reconstruction, not an assertion that original sources never existed.
The seven benchmark scopes are consumer goods, development programs, healthcare,
infant nutrition, maternal health, pharmaceuticals and public institutions. Four
public-reality configuration scopes are healthcare, infant nutrition, maternal
health and pharmaceuticals. Original bytes and approval history are preserved;
separate retirement receipts and reviewed integrity anchors record disposition.
These scopes remain unavailable until separately reviewed replacement evidence
exists. A retirement is not an attestation or new calibration approval.

Full health reports `retired_records` separately from `quarantined_records` and
`attested_records`. `inventory_disposition_complete` means every inventoried
record has passed configuration attestation or has a verified governed retirement.
It does not mean `full_corpus_attested`. With retirements present, the latter
remains false. HTTP 200 with `status=ready_with_retirements` means active inventory
and required infrastructure are ready, with no unresolved dispositions. Its
controlling signal is `inventory_operational_ready`. Clients requiring the entire
historical corpus must continue to check `full_corpus_attested` explicitly.

## Permission and review boundaries

For applicable public-reality assessments, an explicit relevant permission denial
or adverse regulatory flag cannot be averaged away by positive indicators.
An affirmative observation must be current, dated and scoped to the requested
country and supported entity/country scope. Missing qualifying permission caps
the public-reality band; a positive caller assertion does not authenticate the
source or authorize execution. This is an engineering safety policy, not a
jurisdiction-specific legal conclusion.

Reviewer-input preflight is not expert validation. Repeated reviewer identities,
missing required threshold tiers and rankings with no variation cannot establish
the required review agreement. Real qualifications, conflicts, blinded responses,
source rights and sealed safety cases need accountable review. A replacement
study also needs a defined population, sampling unit, ownership-cluster handling,
preregistered holdout and justified uncertainty. Neither a fixed entity count nor
a country count alone proves adequacy or pan-African representativeness.
