## What changes for integrators

- An effect whose linked records state only support or demand is `insufficient`, however the claims are grouped.
- `required_dependencies: []` no longer yields `favorable` on support or demand alone.
- `/v1/privacy-risk-review`, `/v1/evidence-validation` and `/v1/claim-audit` can change `status` and clearance fields when a finding blocks. Read `route_audit_result.controlling`.
- A licensed REST first call now returns a single-use MCP continuation. Use it under the same response profile.

## What this does not establish

- The evaluator's gates are unproven until its frozen cohort and audit cases are rerun. Its complete, irrelevant-addition and conditional wording is still unseen, so the 456/480 gate may not follow from our results.
- The audit clean-control wording behind its false positives is unseen.
- Record classing is rule-based. `required_dependencies` remains the exact route when a caller knows the prerequisite.
- We observed a real throttle. We did not observe a transient server failure, and we did not induce one.
- Receivers here are AMOS-operated. Registry succession and signed statements use the conformance realm and carry no production authority.
- The model comparison and the programme change-control pilot have not run. They stay locked until the evaluator's gates pass.
