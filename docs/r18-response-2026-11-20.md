## What this does not establish

- Your gates are unproven until your frozen cohort and audit cases are rerun.
- The completion check is deterministic English rules. It reads the issuer as the body named before the act verb, and it needs a dated record. A completion worded without either stays open. It holds rather than permits.
- Closing a named step changes the analytical state only. Execution still needs the separately tested authority path.
- Receivers here are AMOS-operated. The model comparison and the commercial pilot remain gated under your criteria.
