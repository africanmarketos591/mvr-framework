## What changes for integrators

- An effect resting only on stakeholder support or demand is now `insufficient` until a completion or permission claim is supplied. `evidence_request` is exempt.
- A licensed key on REST `/v1/first-call` now needs `entity_archetype` and an `evidence_pack` before a workflow starts, as licensed MCP already did. The route stays public without a key.
- `requested_effects` that are not an array of objects with unique `effect_id` values are rejected.

## What this does not establish

- The evaluator's gates are unproven until its private cohort and audit cases are rerun.
- Claim classification is rule-based. `required_dependencies` is the exact route when a caller knows the dependency.
- The signed applicability statement uses the Enterprise conformance root. It carries no production authority. A production signing key still needs the witnessed key ceremony.
- All receivers here are AMOS-operated or evaluator-operated. No buyer-owned adapter has been tested.
- We did not find the cause of the earlier 503 or the pre-HTTP failures. Our no-retry runs came from one client on one day.
- Reviewer assurance remains tenant-declared.
- The model comparison and the controlled commercial pilot have not run.
