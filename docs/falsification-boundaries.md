# MVR evidence and evaluation boundaries

Updated 2026-09-25. MVR organizes declared evidence and provides advisory relational assessments. It does not issue legal permission to operate.

## What the outputs establish

- Public preflight checks supplied structure, source-reference overlap, declared geography/dates and explicit claim links. It does not read or authenticate the original documents, infer all legal requirements, check official registers, or authorize execution.
- Licensed scoring requires suitable measurements, samples, provenance and applicable calibration. Permission-document summaries alone do not provide numerical relational measurements.
- source_review_requirements.input_assessment_scope reports this distinction. Missing measurements are not proof that a permit is absent, invalid or refused. Do not invent scores, respondent counts or claim links to obtain an affirmative result.
- Country-level documents remain available for source review. A national instrument may have a relevant local remit, but this must be checked against the holder, activity, conditions and current law. Excluding national metadata from a local relational score does not invalidate that instrument, and national metadata is not a substitute for local observations.
- A guardian or administrative source label does not establish competent consent. Check whether the legally required body, assembly or authority actually decided the specific matter. A chief, committee, commercial partner or representative must not silently substitute for that body.

## Verification labels

Positive verification labels on entity-reported sources no longer satisfy public verified coverage. Such original items are retained, not deleted. In licensed decision checks, decision-relevant self-labelled claims or measurements require review. Add genuine attributable external corroboration and preserve its relationship to the original source. Do not relabel an internal record as external or invent a verifier. Other admitted labels remain caller declarations, not AMOS authentication.

## A bounded human permission review

1. Name the acting legal entity, exact activity, place, date and decision boundary.
2. Have a competent accountable reviewer enumerate applicable requirements and cite current primary sources, including exceptions and required consents. Do not assume a supplied list is exhaustive.
3. Check each original instrument's issuer, holder, competent consenting body, territorial and activity scope, conditions, expiry, suspension and superseding notices.
4. Record supported, missing, disputed and unknown requirements separately, with source references, reviewer role, review date and unresolved limitations. Keep private identifiers in authorized records.
5. The accountable human decides within actual authority. A completed checklist, structurally complete pack, advisory verdict or paid key does not grant execution permission.

## Performance claims

Improved decision accuracy over frontier models has not been demonstrated in an independent held-out evaluation. Internal lab backtests, MVR-Bench fixtures, publication mirrors and usage counts are not substitutes for that evidence. The current outcome-calibration publication state is available at https://africanmarketos.com/.well-known/mvr-outcome-calibration.json.

A small constructed test with no baseline errors can show no observed improvement on that sample. It cannot establish general equivalence, calibrated abstention or absence of value on other decisions. Score non-authorizing routing tools as routing tools; mapping every non-authorizing result to a failed launch decision predetermines zero GO coverage. Do not equate permissions with quantitative relational readiness.

Use independent adjudication, held-out cases, an information-matched strong-model baseline, generic-checklist and caution controls, repeated runs, cost and error rates at matched answer coverage. Preregister labels, exclusions and stopping rules. Once cases and labels have been exposed to developers, use them for regression only, not as a fresh blind benchmark. Predictive claims additionally require prospective, independently adjudicated outcomes with time-stamped comparator predictions; do not retrofit historical predictions or treat a minimum publication-count gate as comparative proof.

No new legal registry, statutory document extractor, automatic legal GO outcome or independent evaluation result is claimed by this update.
