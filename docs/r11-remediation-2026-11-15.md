# Enterprise R11 remediation and qualification protocol

Candidate revision `2026-11-15.exact-reducer.4` addresses the independent Enterprise evaluation of `v6.32.0 / 2026-11-14.exact-reducer.3`. The evaluator awarded 85.39/100 for supervised use, 49.13/100 for portable permission infrastructure (HOLD), and 35/100 for commercial evidence.

## Migrating an authority-bearing effect

Use the bound executor to call `check_permission` for its case, ISO country and complete executable effect. A fully permitted exact check returns `permission_position.exact_effect_binding.sha256` and a validity limit. Supply that digest as `authority_binding_sha256` on the outer effect. Independently supply its action, effect ID, explicit country, amount in minor units and currency, resource, contact flag and counterparty. The decision rechecks the current authenticated capability and records the outer effect hash. Clients can carry the normative decision hash and the two binding hashes through a handoff. An incomplete legacy request or a scope the signed wire cannot represent stays unauthorized. The standard five-stage analytical workflow issues no execution authority.

## Buyer and commercial evidence

Receiver evidence must identify the operator, receiver, release and commit identifiers. A buyer-owned durable record must show the successful control and the revoked attempt, with the write count for each identifier. Evaluator-controlled markers and AMOS-run receivers establish different evidence. Independent requalification, buyer evidence, model comparison and comparative commercial results remain outstanding at protocol freeze.

After the security and state gates pass, the comparison calls for at least 120 cases, 40% unseen, six markets and four workflows, with matched no-model and same-model arms. Measure critical misses, false holds, bounded closure, review effort, closure time, reconstruction, revocation delay, integration hours, latency and cost. Keep the model and commercial status at HOLD until their prerequisites and measurements are documented.
