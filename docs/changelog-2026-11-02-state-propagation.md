# Changelog: 2026-11-02.state-propagation.2

`2026-11-02.state-propagation.1` was deployed at 20:23 UTC on 2 November and superseded the next day. Its R9 qualification passed live (effect sets A and B exact in both profiles, the held-out audit bar in both profiles, relevance on every route), but the licensed favourable-path regression found that a permission statement issued for a modelled effect no longer validated against the closed published contract: `effect_state` now carries `canonical_action_id` and `modelled_as`, which the published statement schema did not allow. In `.2` the schema allows both fields (optional, so earlier statements still validate) and the private CI checks the closed schema directly. Its manifest stays in `release-manifests/` as the deployed record of `.1`.

This release answers the R9 Enterprise qualification. R9 found that the three-axis envelope held at the safety boundary, but that the parts were not yet one trustworthy loop: analytical state was falsely favorable for missing and conditional evidence, accountable dispositions did not change effect state, audit routes were weak, the effect and authority layers disagreed on action identity, and the published contracts were not self-consistent. Its principle is deterministic state propagation: read each record's stated state once, recompute only the dependent exact effects, use one action identity, distribute current state, and let a buyer-controlled receiver enforce the result.

## P0: correctness

- **Effect-specific sufficiency (P0.1).** A new shared rule set, `mvr.support-state/1`, reads what each record declared as support says about the link it should establish: established, missing link (pending, applied for, awaiting, draft, unsigned, not issued, not found, intended), qualified (subject to, provided that, limited to, provisional, valid only within, the first N), adverse (revoked, suspended, cancelled, expired, refused, terminated, prohibited from) or ambiguous. It guards negation, boilerplate ("subject to the laws of Kenya"), names ("Maji Foods Limited to supply"), later completions and renewals. Support for a claim that an instrument or relationship exists must state it established; silence or unfamiliar wording is a missing link, never support. Precedence `mvr.effect-precedence/2`: a missing link outweighs support it does not post-date (R3); a qualifier makes the claim conditional even beside unqualified support (R4); internal planning is favorable and planning_only and relies on no market claim (R0_INTERNAL_PLANNING), so a revocation never makes planning adverse. A permission revocation stated in prose now reaches the authority axis as `revoked`. Conformance: `conformance/effect-decision/r9-heldout/` (set A first pass 229/240 with 8 false favorable, then 240/240; set B first pass 64/80 with 16 false favorable, then 80/80).
- **Reviewer disposition propagation (P0.2).** One routing rule for every label pair: a reading routes to review when it is adverse or qualifies, when its label differs from the label its declared relationship implies, or when readings disagree. A disposition must name its reviewer (`reviewer {reviewer_id, ...}`, `identity_assurance: declared_unverified`), binds the reading receipts it answers, and is itself a receipt-bound record (`disposition_id`, `disposition_sha256`); a later disposition supersedes it and `withdraw_disposition` ends it. Effect decisions bind dispositions by the exact claim text and passage they were made on (SHA-256), never by caller IDs alone; a changed passage never inherits a disposition. Every effect decision lists the dispositions applied and unmatched and `changed_effect_ids`, and each changed effect carries `changed_by_dispositions.before`. Dispositions stay possible for 30 days after the two-hour submission window. A disposition changes analytical state only, never authority, standing or execution.
- **One action ontology (P0.3).** Every action-ontology ID and immutable legacy alias resolves to one `canonical_action_id` and one effect type, in the effect engine, the authority chain (capability scope, review targets, covering capability, ceilings, prohibitions), the lab harmless-effect check and the buyer verifier. `create_draft_record` and `amos.document.draft.create@1` are the same action everywhere; `REVIEW_EFFECT_ALIAS_MISMATCH` is retired; an authority reference for another action is `AUTHORITY_REF_ACTION_MISMATCH`; an unknown `amos.*` ID is `EFFECT_ACTION_UNKNOWN`, never classified from prose. `schedule_meeting` now carries the ontology meaning (a meeting with an outside party).
- **Route-level audit contract (P0.4).** The audit module reads every shape its routes accept (free text, report and release packs, decision results, red-team outputs, `available_data`, single-record evidence reviews with inline claims) and covers three more routes: `/v1/evidence-suitability-scan`, `/v1/red-team`, `/v1/evidence-review`. New checks: record validation without claims, stated missing links and qualifiers, an output that asserts approval or readiness beyond the evidence (`OUTPUT_OVERSTATES_EVIDENCE`), AMOS cited as the source of permission (`OUTPUT_CITES_AMOS_AS_PERMISSION`), and permission claims from self-reported or press sources (`SOURCE_UNSUITABLE_FOR_USE`). Clean-case false positives removed: no analysis-date finding on routes that take no analysis date, organisational contacts are not personal data, common date and geography spellings are read. Conformance: `conformance/audit-findings/heldout-r9/` (first pass 14/16, then 16/16, 0 clean false positives, 24/24 controlling, 16/16 targeted, both profiles).
- **One relevance router (P0.5).** `mvr.relevance-router/1` is used by first call (licensed and public), guided preflight, every audit route and the effect route. Internal non-market work stops `not_relevant` whatever country, market or sector is attached; an attached claim that states an external effect keeps the request relevant, so a routine label cannot hide a decision; mixed requests split.
- **Self-consistent client contracts (P0.6).** `/v1/capabilities` names the eight licensed MCP tools `tools/list` returns; the exact-effect and submission tools publish their detailed output schemas; every OpenAPI 429 declares `Retry-After` and `RateLimit-Limit/Remaining/Reset`, and every runtime 429 sends them.

## P1: portable organisational permission

- **Enterprise conformance realm (P1.1).** A licensed Enterprise key opens a tenant-bound session in `amos-enterprise-conformance`, signed by its own root (`/.well-known/mvr-conformance-trust-root.json`, pinned in `trust/`), with `recognized_standing: none` and `production_authority: false`. `/v1/effect-decision` reads it through `authority_ref.conformance_session_id`, so one positive exact capability qualifies end to end. See `conformance/enterprise-conformance/`.
- **Signed current-state feed and offline policy (P1.2).** Every registration, revocation, closure and session close is a hash-chained entry in a signed feed (`GET /v1/authority-conformance/feed`); `wait` holds the request open, so a connected receiver learns of a revocation within seconds, and a receiver that was offline catches up from its last sequence. Current-state proofs carry `risk_class`, `feed_sequence` and `feed_head_sha256`; `mvr.offline-policy/1` sets low 300 s, standard 60 s, high 5 s.
- **Buyer-executor verifier (P1.3).** `sdks/javascript/buyer-executor-verifier.mjs` with `conformance/receiver-kit/feed-subscriber.mjs` and an offline negative suite of 28 cases (`conformance/buyer-executor/`).
- **Verification ledger (P1.4).** Each record now carries passage anchors (field, claim and passage SHA-256), the support-state reading, attributed model readings, issuer signature and registry status as declared and not checked, declared retrieval and custody, independent corroboration, dispositions with reviewer and assurance, and the withdrawal source (superseding record, stated or structured withdrawal).

## Compatibility

- Effect decisions change where support stated a missing link or a qualifier, where a qualifier stood beside direct support, and for internal planning under adverse records. Normative digests include `canonical_action_id`.
- Dispositions require `reviewer`; the response carries `disposition_receipt`; assurance is `tenant_declared_reviewer` or `tenant_declared_same_principal_as_issuer`.
- `schedule_meeting` resolves to `external_meeting`; the internal alias is now `schedule_internal_meeting`.
