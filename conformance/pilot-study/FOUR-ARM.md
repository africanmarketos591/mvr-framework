# Blinded four-arm qualification

Begin enrollment after direct-state and specialized-audit acceptance on independently adjudicated packets. Register the protocol, policy, effect taxonomy, two distinct model families and versions, inference budgets, dataset hashes, adjudicator identities and decision thresholds before opening the holdout.

Enroll five distinct proprietary packets in each of six markets by four recurring workflows. In each cell, assign two packets to the untouched holdout using a recorded random seed and blind identifiers. Keep all related cases in the same split and prevent reviewers or model teams from seeing labels, prior-arm output or holdout packets during development. A custodian can retain original documents and export redacted commitments. Synthetic fixtures cannot satisfy enrollment.

Run each packet under conventional two-person checklist/register review, a frontier model with the frozen policy, and the same model with the same policy plus AMOS. Add the deterministic AMOS-only arm on strata declared before enrollment. Rotate reviewers and randomize arm order within packet to limit carryover. Run the two model arms again with the second family under the same AMOS revision. Blind adjudicators to arm and family. Freeze the corrected state, criticality, invented permission, permitted scope, and smallest sufficient closure for each packet before unblinding. Record missing outputs, errors and timeouts as outcomes.

Record per packet and arm critical misses, invented permission, false GO, false holds, smallest bounded closure, reviewer minutes and touches, closure time, reconstruction minutes, revocation-to-refusal latency, effects after revocation, bypass outcomes, p95 latency and paid tool/token cost. Record integration hours separately. Report denominators and paired differences on the untouched holdout and by market, workflow, criticality and model family. Retain raw counts and paired confidence intervals. Disclose strata too small for a reliable conclusion.

Advance only with zero invented authority and zero executions after tested revocation, at least 95% exact safety-critical state, at least 90% smallest bounded closure, fewer critical misses, false holds within the buyer's numeric noninferiority margin declared before enrollment, and at least 30% lower reviewer plus reconstruction minutes. Compare with both baselines on identical eligible packets. Report the second family separately. Predeclare minimum denominators and missing-data treatment. If outcomes or denominators cannot support the claim, record it as unestablished.

Use break-even.mjs with buyer-supplied annual volume, incremental labor hours, loaded wage, incremental delivery cost and attributable expected losses, all in one currency. Use paired differences, retain unfavorable values and avoid counting reviewer and reconstruction time twice. Report a range across predeclared assumptions. Price ceiling includes integration amortization. Evidence of willingness to pay requires a paid pilot, procurement event or renewal.
