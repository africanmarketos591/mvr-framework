# Exact-effect qualification

Each of four sets contains 40 complete statements, 40 missing links and 40 qualified statements. Each request also contains an internal planning control. The 32 structure cases vary linkage, dates, declared relationships and authority state.

| Set | First-pass exact sentences | Final regression |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| C | 75/120 on production .3 | 120/120 |
| D | 104/120 on candidate, 66/120 on production .3 | 120/120 |
| E | 86/120 on candidate | 120/120 |
| F | 107/120 on candidate | 120/120 |

Sets D, E and F were first scored before the changes each prompted. Together those first passes scored 297/360 (82.5%). Post-fix scores describe regression coverage. The outside evaluator's unseen-case accuracy remains unmeasured. The reader is deterministic and reviewer dispositions are bound to the exact claim and record.

Rebuild with node conformance/effect-decision/r10-heldout/build.mjs. The worker suite scripts/test-exact-reducer-r10.mjs runs the frozen inputs through the reducer and also verifies disposition withdrawal and planning scope.

A subsequent 36-sentence cohort (fresh-36.json) scored 28/36 on its first pass. Five missing links were labeled favorable and three positive records were held. No external effect was authorized. The eight cases passed after the reader changes. The first-pass results are preserved in fresh-36-first-pass.json.
